Quick Fuel Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Quick Fuel Check collects no personal data. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The only thing the app saves is which calculator you used last, kept on your iPhone or iPad in the app's own storage. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app. Fuel figures, distances and other numbers you type are not saved at all.

Apple

If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass anonymous crash and usage statistics to the developer. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. Downloads and updates through the App Store are also handled by Apple.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
