What the app does

An offline fuel calculator for pilots: unit conversion (litres, US gallons, kilograms, pounds) at your fuel density, trip fuel with reserve, endurance, and a reference card for jerry cans, drums and long range tanks. Choose a calculator with the wheel on the right edge; type with the app's own keypad.

Common questions

Why are my numbers gone when I reopen the app? On purpose. Fuel belongs to the flight, so every launch starts fresh. Only the last calculator you used is remembered.

How do I change the fuel density? Tap the density field at the top and type it. The default is 0.800 kg/L.

Does it need internet? No. Everything works offline.

Important

Advisory only. Not for navigation. Quick Fuel Check is not a substitute for the approved flight manual, the aircraft's fuel indications or your operator's procedures. Check every figure before flight.
