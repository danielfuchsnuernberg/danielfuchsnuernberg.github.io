What the app does

An offline flight computer: altitude (pressure, density, true), wind correction, crosswind, true airspeed, groundspeed/distance/time, weight check, flight time (FAA and PNG), and conversions. Choose a calculator with the wheel on the right edge.

Common questions

Where are my aircraft weights? Only on your device: Settings → Aircraft. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I change units? Settings: pressure, temperature, altitude, distance and speed. Numbers you already typed are converted.

Does it need internet? No. Everything works offline.

Important

Advisory only. E6B is not a substitute for the approved flight manual or your operator's procedures. Always cross-check with approved data.
